Privacy Policy

PickUp UCF helps students organize and join pickup sports sessions. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

Effective September 14, 2026

The short version

We use your information to run PickUp UCF. We do not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, username, UCF email address, account identifier, and preferred sports.
  • Session information: games you create or join, sport, time, skill level, capacity, attendance, venue, and custom session coordinates when you choose a map location.
  • User content: session notes, chat messages, reports, and other information you choose to submit.
  • Device information: notification and Live Activity tokens used to deliver app functionality.
  • Product interactions: actions needed to operate features such as joining, leaving, blocking, reporting, notification preferences, and account state.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • create and secure accounts and verify eligible UCF email addresses;
  • display, organize, and manage pickup sports sessions;
  • personalize sport-related app features;
  • send service emails, notifications, reminders, and Live Activity updates;
  • support safety features, including blocking and reporting; and
  • maintain the reliability and security of the service.

Device permissions

  • Location: used when you ask to center the map on your position. A custom game location you submit is stored with that session.
  • Calendar: used only when you choose to add or remove a game in your device calendar. The app stores the identifier of an event it created on your device so it can avoid duplicates and remove that event if the game is cancelled.
  • Notifications and Live Activities: used when enabled to provide session updates and reminders.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Service providers

PickUp UCF uses service providers only to operate app functionality:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, and server functions;
  • Brevo for account verification and password-reset emails;
  • Apple Push Notification service for notifications and Live Activities; and
  • Open-Meteo for weather forecasts associated with session coordinates.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments, as applicable to the services they provide.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information. We do not use information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Information may be visible to other users when needed for app features—for example, profile details, session details, participant status, and messages shared in a session. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and comply with obligations for the service. You can request account deletion from the app’s profile settings or by contacting us. Deletion removes or de-identifies account-linked information unless limited retention is required for security, legal, or operational reasons. Residual copies may remain temporarily in protected backups.

Your choices

You may update profile details and notification preferences in the app, revoke device permissions in iOS Settings, leave sessions, delete your account, or contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion.

Security and age

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. PickUp UCF is intended for eligible UCF community members and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will revise the effective date on this page and provide additional notice when appropriate.
