Common questions

I can’t sign in or verify my email

Use an eligible @ucf.edu or @knights.ucf.edu address. Check your inbox and spam folder for verification mail. If you forgot your password, use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen. Contact support if you still can’t access your account.

I’m not getting game or chat alerts

Check Profile → Settings → Notification settings in PickUp UCF, then check the app’s notification permission in iPhone Settings. Focus and Scheduled Summary can affect when alerts appear. If you contact support, include the approximate time of the missing alert and your time zone.

My calendar still shows an edited or cancelled game

Adding a game to Calendar creates an independent copy. It does not update when the host edits or cancels the game. Check the latest details in PickUp UCF and edit or remove your calendar copy as needed.

I need to report someone or block contact

Use the report controls on the relevant game, message, or profile. You can manage blocks in Profile → Settings → Blocked users. For additional help, contact support without sharing unnecessary personal information.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account and review the confirmation. The Privacy Policy explains deletion and retention. Contact support if you can’t access the app.

Urgent safety concerns

PickUp UCF is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, call 911 or UCF Police. See the Community Guidelines for reporting and response information.
