PickUp UCF

Community Guidelines

Effective September 16, 2026

Respect other players

Do not post harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, bullying, impersonation, or targeted abuse.

Keep it safe and relevant

Do not post scams, spam, illegal activity, unsafe meetup instructions, graphic content, or another person’s private information. Use session notes and chat to coordinate pickup sports.

Reports and blocks

Report a message, user, or session that breaks these rules. Blocking prevents contact and hides content between both accounts. Existing membership and chat records may be retained for integrity, abuse review, and legal or safety needs.

Enforcement

We may remove content, warn or suspend accounts, preserve evidence, and refer credible threats or illegal conduct to appropriate authorities. False or abusive reporting may itself lead to action.

Support and emergencies

We aim to acknowledge urgent safety reports within 24 hours and other reports within 72 hours. PickUp UCF is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, call 911 or UCF Police.
